Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · This notice is provided under Articles 13 & 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / AVG).

Etaros ("Etaros", "we", "us") is a security tool that helps you detect scams, phishing, and fraud. Protecting your data is core to what we do. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, on what legal basis, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have.

1. Who is responsible (data controller)

Etaros is an independent project run by a private individual based in the Netherlands. The controller responsible for your personal data can be reached at:

  • Project: Etaros
  • Email: etaros.app@gmail.com

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as we are not required to under Article 37 GDPR. For any privacy question, or to receive our full contact details, use the email above.

3. Where your screen data goes

Analysis runs on Mistral AI, on European infrastructure. Your screen is not sent to Google, Meta or Microsoft. Mistral does not train models on API data and holds API traffic for 30 days for abuse prevention before deleting it; we hold nothing at any point.

The screen is only sent when the question is about the screen. A general question — "how does 2FA work?" — travels as text alone, with no image attached. Automatic scanning sends a frame only while you have monitoring switched on.

Each image is analysed to produce a verdict and then discarded. We keep no history of your screen, no profile and no record of your habits.

You can switch this off entirely. Local mode runs the analysis on your own machine through Ollama; nothing leaves the device, and it is free on every plan.

3a. Use of artificial intelligence

Etaros is an AI product and we say so plainly, as Article 50 of the EU AI Act requires: you are interacting with an AI system, not a human reviewer. No person at Etaros reads your screen or your conversations.

The AI can be wrong in both directions: it will sometimes miss a real threat and sometimes flag something harmless. Its verdicts are guidance to help you decide, never a decision made about you, and you should keep your own judgement. Etaros performs no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.

5. International transfers

Your screen content does not leave the European Economic Area. Analysis (Mistral), your account (Supabase) and crash reports (Sentry) all stay in the EU by design, and that is the point of the architecture rather than a side effect.

Three processors sit outside the EEA and none of them touches your screen: Formspree (the website waitlist form), Groq (the demo on the website, never the installed app) and Stripe (payments, not active yet). Where data reaches them it is protected by appropriate safeguards under Articles 44-46 GDPR — the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards via the contact email.

If you enter your own Anthropic or Google API key in Settings, requests go to that provider under your own agreement with them. That is off by default.

6. Cookies and local storage

The website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The live demo stores a small counter in your browser's local storage to enforce the free-trial limit. This is strictly necessary for that feature and stores no personal data. You can clear it any time via your browser.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectify inaccurate data;
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restrict or object to processing;
  • Data portability (receive your data in a portable format);
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority - in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

To exercise any right, email etaros.app@gmail.com. We respond within one month (Art. 12(3) GDPR).

8. Children

Accounts are intended for users aged 16 or older (the digital-consent age in the Netherlands). If you are under 16, a parent or guardian must consent on your behalf.

9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and limiting who can access account data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and will notify you and the supervisory authority of a breach where legally required.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
